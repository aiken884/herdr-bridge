# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
the herdr-bridge project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainers through GitHub (open a private report via the
repository's Security tab, or contact a maintainer directly). All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

# 貢獻者行為準則

## 我們的承諾

身為 herdr-bridge 專案的成員、貢獻者與維護者,我們承諾讓每一個人都能在這個專
案中不受騷擾地參與,無論年齡、體型、可見或不可見的身心障礙、族裔、性徵、性別
認同與表達、經驗多寡、教育背景、社經地位、國籍、外貌、種族、宗教信仰,或性傾
向與性別認同為何。

## 我們的標準

有助於建立正向環境的行為,例如:
- 對他人展現同理心與善意
- 尊重不同的意見、觀點與經驗
- 願意提出建設性回饋,也能大方接受他人的回饋
- 為自己的錯誤負責,並向受影響的人致歉
- 以社群整體的最佳利益為優先考量

不被接受的行為,例如:
- 使用具性暗示的言語或圖像,以及任何形式的性騷擾或性挑逗
- 酸言酸語、人身攻擊、貶低他人的言論,以及針對個人或政治立場的攻擊
- 公開或私下的騷擾行為
- 未經當事人明確同意,公開他人的私人資訊
- 其他在專業場合中可合理認定為不恰當的行為

## 執行方式

若遭遇濫用、騷擾或其他不當行為,可透過 GitHub 向維護者回報(可經由 repository
的 Security 分頁提出私下回報,或直接聯繫維護者)。所有申訴都會被及時、公正地
審查與調查。
